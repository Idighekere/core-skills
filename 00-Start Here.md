## Mental model

Notes fall into four categories, and the connections between them are the point:

- **Data Structures** — the containers. *"How is it stored in memory?"*
- **Patterns** — the reusable moves. *"When do I reach for this and how do I recognise it?"*
- **Concepts** — the principles and trade-offs. *"Why does this work / when does it fail?"*
- **Problems** — the journal. *"Here's a problem I solved, the mistake I made, and the insight."*

A problem note links *up* to its patterns and data structures; those notes link *down* to related problems.
